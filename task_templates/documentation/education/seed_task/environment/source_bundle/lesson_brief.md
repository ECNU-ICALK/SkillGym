# Global Education Cohort Workshop

This workshop is for early-career education policy analysts who are comfortable reading CSV files and basic charts but still need practice turning raw indicator extracts into a clean teaching notebook.

The teaching goal is to compare one compact peer cohort across three dimensions:
- attainment depth
- upper-secondary access
- public spending effort

The cohort for the lesson is defined in `country_cohort.csv`. Only rows with `include_in_lesson = yes` belong in the final lesson outputs.

The notebook should help an instructor guide learners through:
1. reading the three source extracts,
2. reconciling column names and indicator labels,
3. aligning the cohort to one common comparison year,
4. contrasting cross-sectional results with a short recent trend window,
5. ending with evidence-backed takeaways and caveats.

The final lesson should stay descriptive. It may compare patterns across the cohort, but it should not prescribe reforms, infer causality, or introduce outside indicators.
