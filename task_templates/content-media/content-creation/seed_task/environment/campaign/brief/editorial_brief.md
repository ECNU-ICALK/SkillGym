# Editorial Brief

Campaign title: Renewable Capacity Momentum 2025

Primary audience:
- climate-tech operators, policy teams, and business readers who need a concise public-facing summary

Core briefing arc:
- 2024 delivered record renewable capacity growth, which settles the scale question.
- Current government ambitions still sit below the 2030 tripling benchmark, so the delivery gap remains material.
- Grid investment, permitting, financing, and wider rollout capacity now determine whether momentum can hold.

Channel goals:
- Newsletter opener: sound like the front end of a sharper analyst briefing, not a generic explainer.
- LinkedIn post: extract one operating takeaway for business readers who want a practical read rather than a long recap.
- Thread: move in six compact steps from record additions to the remaining 2030 gap and the execution bottlenecks, using step 3 for the solar-led growth engine before step 4 narrows to the China concentration point.
- Video script: follow a visual progression from record build, to generation trend, to the unresolved delivery gap.

Delivery rules:
- Keep the campaign theme consistent across all outputs.
- Do not repeat the same opening move or the same closing move everywhere.
- Use only supported numbers and phrasing from the packaged evidence.
- Treat updated packaged inputs as the source of truth on reruns.
