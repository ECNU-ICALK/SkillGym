# Channel Voice Notes

Newsletter
- Sound like the opening section of a sharper analyst briefing.
- Use one house-style anchor naturally.
- Keep paragraphs tight and proof-led.

LinkedIn
- Write for a broader business reader.
- Pull one operating takeaway from the same evidence pack.
- End with a statement, not a question.

Thread
- Exactly six additive steps.
- Move briskly from record scale to the remaining gap and the practical constraint.
- Use post 3 to establish solar as the leading growth engine before post 4 narrows to the China concentration snapshot.
- Avoid hashtags and filler transitions.

Video
- Write for six fast scenes with clear visual progression.
- Keep `On-screen text` tighter than `Voiceover`.
- Do not narrate the same sentence structure used in the thread.
