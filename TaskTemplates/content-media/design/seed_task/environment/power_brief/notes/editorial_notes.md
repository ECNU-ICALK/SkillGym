# Editorial Notes

- Tone: concise, analytical, and briefing-ready.
- Audience: internal review readers who want cross-country comparison quickly.
- Preferred phrasing: use direct comparisons, year markers, and source-aware wording.
- Avoid filler such as placeholder copy, broad slogans, and generic calls to action.

Source references:
- World Bank country metadata: https://api.worldbank.org/v2/country/USA;CAN;MEX?format=json&per_page=100
- World Bank population indicator: https://api.worldbank.org/v2/country/USA;CAN;MEX/indicator/SP.POP.TOTL?format=json&per_page=20000
- World Bank GDP indicator: https://api.worldbank.org/v2/country/USA;CAN;MEX/indicator/NY.GDP.MKTP.CD?format=json&per_page=20000
- Our World in Data annual CO2 emissions: https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/annual-co2-emissions-per-country.csv?v=1&csvType=full&useColumnShortNames=false
- Our World in Data electricity production by source: https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/electricity-prod-source-stacked.csv?v=1&csvType=full&useColumnShortNames=false

Formatting notes:
- Keep each page self-contained and readable at briefing pace.
- Put chart explanation near the chart it refers to.
- Keep the appendix factual and compact.
- For the implications page, make the three cards explicitly cover GDP scale, the lowest latest annual CO2 total, and the latest clean-generation lead.
- In the manifest, keep each page's data-file list tight to that page, and keep notes focused on delivery/data context instead of runtime commentary.
