# Metric Notes

## Included indicator families

- `mean_years_schooling`: average years of schooling for both genders.
- `gross_upper_secondary_enrolment_pct`: gross enrolment ratio for upper secondary education.
- `education_spending_pct_gdp`: total education spending across all levels as a share of GDP.

## Units

- `mean_years_schooling` uses years.
- `gross_upper_secondary_enrolment_pct` uses percent and can exceed 100 because it is a gross enrolment ratio.
- `education_spending_pct_gdp` uses percent of GDP.

## Time coverage notes

- The three extracts do not always end in the same calendar year for every entity.
- The lesson must identify the latest year that is shared by every included entity across all three indicators before making a same-year cohort comparison.
- Recent trend discussion should stay inside the 2018-2022 window when data are available for the included entities.
